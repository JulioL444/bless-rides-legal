How to delete your account in the app

Users can request account deletion directly inside the Bless Rides mobile app.

  1. Open the Bless Rides mobile app.
  2. Sign in with your Google account.
  3. Go to Profile.
  4. Select Eliminar cuenta.
  5. Follow the confirmation steps shown in the app.

How to request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, you may request account deletion by email.

Send your request to: panaisolutions@gmail.com

Please include:

  • Your full name.
  • The email address used to sign in to Bless Rides.
  • Your phone number registered in the app, if available.
  • A clear statement that you want to delete your Bless Rides account and associated data.

What data may be deleted

When an account deletion request is processed, Bless Rides may delete or anonymize account data associated with the user, including:

  • Profile information such as name, email, phone number and profile photo.
  • Account role and app profile information.
  • Passenger or driver profile data, when applicable.
  • Uploaded photos, selfies, verification images or documents, when no longer required.
  • Chat or support information associated with the account, when eligible for deletion.
  • Device notification tokens associated with the account.

Data that may be retained

Some information may be retained when necessary for legal, safety, fraud prevention, financial, tax, regulatory, dispute resolution, or security reasons.

This may include:

  • Trip records needed for safety, support, dispute resolution or legal compliance.
  • Payment, wallet, commission, top-up or transaction records needed for accounting or financial obligations.
  • Records related to fraud prevention, abuse prevention, account restrictions, bans or security investigations.
  • Information required by applicable law or valid requests from competent authorities.

Processing time

We will review deletion requests and process them within a reasonable period, unless additional verification or legal retention is required.

Important note

Deleting your account may permanently remove access to your Bless Rides profile, ride history, driver profile, wallet information and other app features.
